Our Story

A Taste of the Silk Road

Arzu brings the rich culinary traditions of Uyghur and Middle Eastern cooking to Mount Prospect. Our recipes are rooted in Central Asian heritage — bold spices, hand-crafted doughs, and techniques refined over generations.

Every plate that leaves our kitchen is made with care and passion. From the hand-pulled laghman noodles to the slow-braised lamb, we take no shortcuts. This is real food, made for real people.
