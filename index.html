Our Story

A Taste of the
Silk Road

Arzu brings the rich culinary traditions of Uyghur and Middle Eastern cooking to Mount Prospect. Our recipes are rooted in Central Asian heritage — bold spices, hand-crafted doughs, and techniques refined over centuries.

Every plate is made with care. From hand-pulled laghman to slow-braised lamb, we take no shortcuts. Real food, made for real people.
